Projects

ML

June – August 2026

SBA Loan Default Risk Prediction

End-to-end pipeline on 329,863 resolved SBA 7(a) loans (2014–2024), joined with economic data. Four classifiers, a time-based split, and 5-fold cross-validation. Tuned gradient boosting with RandomizedSearchCV: 0.917 ROC-AUC and 0.758 PR-AUC on held-out data. Flask app scores new applications into Low / Medium / High tiers (observed default rates 1.6% / 4.7% / 41.1%).

News Headline Analysis

Classified 200K+ headlines into 11 categories with sentence transformers: 82% accuracy and 0.79 F1, about 2 points above a TF-IDF baseline. Used K-Means, HDBSCAN, and t-SNE to surface topic patterns and market themes in a Jupyter notebook.

DJIA Time Series Forecasting

Compared SARIMA and Prophet on 10+ years of DJIA data, with stationarity tests, ACF/PACF, and differencing. Prophet fit the longer trend better: RMSE 1,519 and MAE 1,283, against SARIMA RMSE 2,839.
